01

About

I trained in mathematics, then found the interesting part was never the proof. It was the classroom the proof was supposed to reach.

That is roughly how I ended up here. I founded ODK Solutions Hub to do applied data work for people who cannot staff a data team: small businesses, NGOs, schools, farms. The projects look unrelated from outside, but they run on one question. What does this organisation already measure, and which decision improves if someone models it properly?

My master's thesis models mathematics performance for Ghanaian junior high students, so I spend most of my time on feature engineering against records nobody designed for modelling. The same discipline carries into the poultry and health work. Field data arrives incomplete and the model has to survive that.

Pricing premix fuel at the pumps

Twelve years of monthly pump prices for the subsidised fuel Ghana's canoe fishers run on, modelled with ARIMA and a logistic regression on the months where the price moved. The forecast held exactly for five months into 2022, then missed June by 72 pesewas when the cedi slid. That miss is the most instructive part: an administered price carries no signal about the shock that ends it. Undergraduate dissertation, University of Ghana.

Stack
R · ARIMA · logistic regression
Focus
Forecasting, administered pricing

View the repo Read the dissertation

Breast cancer classifier, served as an API

A logistic regression on the Wisconsin diagnostic set, split into a FastAPI service that holds the model and a Streamlit client that calls it over HTTP. Model and scaler are serialised together, so inference applies the exact transform training used. The dataset is a benchmark and the model is deliberately plain. What the project is actually about is the seam between training and serving, and keeping preprocessing identical on both sides of it.

Stack
FastAPI · Streamlit · scikit-learn · joblib
Focus
Model serving, train and inference parity
